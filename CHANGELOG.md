# Model revisions

- **S8 (Simple)** — Two shells expose the board switches and LEDs. The open access at CASE9 has a rounded rim; the preceding lower shell and fastener layout can be reused.
- **C3 (Deluxe)** — Four independent keycaps and two light pipes are retained by a screw-fastened plate with side-loaded M3 nuts. A coupon and front template support fit checks before printing the full set.
