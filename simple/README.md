# Assemble the S8

Print one of each [shell](STL) in millimetres. Inspect support removal around the recessed deck, POWER guard and RESET ring. [Fastener stack](preview/fastener_stack_guide.png) · [Hole layout](preview/mounting-plan.png) · [Parts list](parts.json)

Use four 16 mm M3 female–female standoffs with at least about 5 mm usable thread at each end, four M3×12 and seven M3×10 button-head screws, and three M3 hex nuts (5.5 mm across flats, at most 2.4 mm thick). Check screw head diameter ≤5.7 mm and height ≤1.8 mm. Screw lengths are measured under the head.

1. Seat the three nuts in the lower shell at CASE9/10/11. Keep the four round LCD recesses free.
2. Place the base board on its supports. Insert four M3×10 screws from underneath into the standoffs above the board. The four core-area supports can be used alone. Optional M3×6 screws and nuts require underside clearance.
3. Install the core board, connect the 40-pin display ribbon and CAN wires, and place the display on the standoffs.
4. Start four M3×12 screws through the cover into the tops of the standoffs. Start the remaining three M3×10 screws into the captive nuts. Approach CASE9 beside the DC connector from the open front.
5. Tighten evenly once the cover seats freely. Check glass clearance, POWER latching, full return of the other three switches, LED clearance and actual plug insertion. Confirm spacer threads are deep enough before applying torque.
