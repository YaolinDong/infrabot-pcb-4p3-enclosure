# Assemble the C3

Print parts 01–09 in millimetres. Use transparent material for both light pipes and finish their end faces. The upper shell and keycaps are exported face down and the lower shell bottom down. Check orientation and supports for the retainer, light pipes and fit parts, especially the light-pipe flanges and thin sections. Fits start from 0.30 mm clearance. Suggested keycap colours: POWER black, STOP red, RESUME green. Parts 10 and 11 let you check the bushings, nut pockets, light apertures and front alignment before printing the shells. [STLs](STL) · [Nut installation](preview/nut_install_steps.png) · [Exploded view](preview/exploded.png) · [Parts list](parts.json)

Use four M3 male–female spacers (16 mm body, nominal 6 mm stud), 15 M3 hex nuts (5.5 mm across flats, maximum 2.4 mm thick), five M3×6 screws including four optional core-area screws, six M3×5, two M3×12 and two M3×30. ISO 7380-1 low button heads fit the nominal geometry. Do not use tall nylon lock nuts or heat-set inserts. Check the four front screw heads are at most 5.8 mm across and 1.8 mm high.

1. Seat 11 nuts in the lower shell. Set the base board on its supports. Install CASE9 and any core-area M3×6 screws after checking clearance. Fasten the four spacers through the board into the lower nuts.
2. Install the core board and 40-pin display ribbon. Place the display on the spacers and fit two M3×5 screws on the right. Check the header, ribbon bend and core component height. Connect and tighten the CAN wires before closing the case.
3. Lay the upper shell face down. Slide four nuts into the retainer pockets. Insert the four labelled keycaps and both pipes, then secure the retainer with four M3×5 screws. Check POWER latch/release and RESET, STOP and RESUME return.
4. Seat the cover and start the two left M3×12 screws into the shared display spacers and the two right M3×30 screws at CASE10/11. Check spacer thread depth. Tighten gently with the glass clear and buttons unloaded.
5. Check insertion and withdrawal of the actual DC, RJ45, USB-C and SWD plugs and the visibility of each indicator.
